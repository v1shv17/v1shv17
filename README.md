## 🧠 Who I Am

I'm a 2nd-year Computer Science student at TMU who got hooked on AI — not from a course, but from actually building with it.

What started as curiosity about how LLMs work turned into building a fully automated multi-agent investment analysis system, a RAG-powered chatbot, and real production data pipelines at my internship. I learn fastest when I'm shipping something real.

Right now I'm a **Data Engineering Intern at Kensington**, building ETL pipelines, managing cloud data infrastructure on Azure, and delivering analytics that have measurably moved business metrics. Outside of work, I spend my time exploring what's possible at the intersection of **data engineering and AI** — agent architectures, workflow automation, LLM-powered tooling.

> 💼 **Actively seeking a Summer 2026 Co-op** — Data Engineer · AI/ML · Software Developer
